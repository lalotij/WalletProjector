Privacy Policy

Wallet Projector — Last updated: July 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Wallet Projector ("the app") handles your information. The short version: the app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data whatsoever.

Information we collect

None. Wallet Projector does not collect any information about you or your device. The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information, and does not access your contacts, location, photos, or any other device data.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter in the app — expenses, accounts, fixed payments, paycheck amount, and extra income — is stored locally on your device only. This information never leaves your device. We have no servers, and we have no access to your data.

If you delete the app, all of its data is deleted with it. You can also erase all data at any time from within the app using Settings → Reset.

Third-party services

Wallet Projector does not use any third-party services. There are no analytics tools, no advertising networks, no crash reporters, and no tracking technologies of any kind. The app does not connect to the internet.

Children's privacy

Since the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children. Wallet Projector is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change. Any future version of the app that handles data differently will make that clear before you update.
