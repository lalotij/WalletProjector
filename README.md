**Description**:
```
Wallet Projector tells you how much money you'll have left each pay period, before payday arrives.

Enter your expenses, fixed payments and paycheck once, and the app projects your next 10 pay periods: what you owe, what you pay and what's left over in each one.

FEATURES

• 10-period projection: see the total to pay and the money available for every upcoming payday at a glance.
• Split expenses: spread a big purchase across 2 to 10 pay periods; edit or delete one installment and the rest adjust automatically to keep the total.
• Accounts and cards: organize expenses by card or account and track each outstanding balance.
• Fixed payments: rent, car, tuition… applied to every pay period, with the option to skip specific ones.
• Extra income: add bonuses or any one-time income to the right pay period.
• Leftovers carry over: if everything is paid by payday and money is left, it automatically rolls into the next period as income.
• Available in English and Spanish.
• Your data is yours: everything is stored only on your device. No accounts, no ads, no data collection.

Perfect for anyone paid biweekly who wants to stop juggling spreadsheets.
```
